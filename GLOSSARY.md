# Support Ticket Management

This context covers requests for support, their assignment and lifecycle, and the records created during that work.

## Language

**Support ticket**:
A request for assistance that is tracked from submission through resolution.
_Avoid_: Case, issue

**Routing**:
The decision that assigns a support ticket to the team responsible for handling it, based on its category and priority.

**Team**:
A group responsible for handling assigned support tickets.

**Notification record**:
A stored record about a ticket event that may inform users; its existence does not mean a message was delivered.
_Avoid_: Delivered notification
